Traces of Time
Editorial & Interactive
2026

Traces of Time is an editorial and interactive design project exploring aging, decay, and rebirth as continuous stages of transformation. The physical publication uses layered booklets, corrosion textures, shifting typography, and red-thread binding to visualize how time leaves traces and reshapes form. The project was later translated into an interactive web experience, where the interface gradually corrodes through scrolling and leads into a 3D network of images connected by red threads. Users can explore, enlarge, and hold images, but interaction accelerates their decay, leaving traces that transfer onto other images and create new visual states. Inspired by ideas of impermanence, interdependence, and cyclical rebirth, the project treats decay not as an ending, but as material for another form.
