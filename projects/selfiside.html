This book is inspired by the film Fight Club. It explores the film’s philosophical ideas, including its critique of capitalism, the loss of individual identity caused by consumer culture, the chaos that follows that loss, and the idea that what is ultimately needed to reclaim one’s true self is a fundamental form of love.
